


Using the invariant mass $P^2 = M^2 c^2$ we have before the collision
\begin{equation*}
    P_\text{before}^2 = P_A^2 = m_A^2 c^2
\end{equation*}
and after the collision
\begin{align*}
    P_\text{after}^2 &= (P_B + P_C)^2 = P_B^2 +P_C^2 + 2 P_B \cdot P_C = m_B^2 c^2 + m_C^2 c^2 + 2 m_B m_C \gamma(v_B) \gamma(v_C) (c, \vec{v}_B) \cdot (c, \vec{v}_C) \\
    &= m_B^2 c^2 + m_C^2 c^2 + 2 m_B m_C \gamma(v_B) \gamma(v_C) (c^2 - \vec{v}_B \cdot \vec{v}_C)
\end{align*}
By conservation of the time component we have
\begin{equation*}
    m_A c = m_B c \gamma(\vec{v}_B) + m_C c \gamma(\vec{v}_C) \iff m_C \gamma(\vec{v}_C) = m_A - m_B \gamma(\vec{v}_B)
\end{equation*}
Therefore
\begin{align*}
    P_\text{after}^2 &= m_B^2 c^2 + m_C^2 c^2 + 2 m_B \gamma(v_B) (m_A - m_B \gamma(\vec{v}_B)) (c^2 - \vec{v}_B \cdot \vec{v}_C)\\
\end{align*}
